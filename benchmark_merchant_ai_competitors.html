Yext provides enterprise brands and partners with a verified Knowledge Graph, local listings distribution, review/reputation management, pages, and visibility intelligence across AI and traditional search. Its public positioning is oriented to large multi-location organizations; Yext states that brands manage 4M+ locations worldwide and that smaller businesses are generally served through reseller partners.[1] [5]

Verified Capabilities

  • Direct distribution of verified business data to 200+ publishers and LLMs, including Google, Apple Maps, Yelp, ChatGPT, and Gemini
  • listings verification and automated correction of errors, duplicates, and publisher overwrites
  • review monitoring across Google, Facebook, Yelp, and 80+ platforms
  • review-request triggers via email, text, or QR code
  • AI-drafted and optionally automatic review responses plus AI sentiment analysis
  • Knowledge Graph connectors, APIs, MCP connectivity, scheduled updates, role-based approvals, audit trails, localized/multilingual entity profiles including Arabic (Saudi Arabia) locale support
  • AI-generated FAQs, bios, descriptions, and responses subject to review/approval.[1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Pricing

Contact sales; Yext states pricing is custom and solution-bundled, driven mainly by location/provider count, selected capabilities, and contract term. Billing is typically annual, with better rates for multi-year commitments; there is no fixed public tier menu, free tier, or standard free trial.[5]

Custom · Custom

KSA Angle

Yext’s help documentation explicitly lists Arabic (Saudi Arabia) as a supported entity-profile locale and says multi-language profiles apply to accounts in all regions, with downstream Search, Pages, and Listings using locale data.[4] Its global, multi-location distribution model and support for Arabic/Saudi locale data make it a relevant adjacent competitor for Saudi chains and regional enterprises, although the cited public pages do not establish Saudi-specific publisher coverage, offices, or customer references.

Strategic Gap

The public product pages emphasize enterprise-scale listings, reviews, structured data, and AI-search visibility rather than an Arabic-first merchant inbox spanning WhatsApp and Instagram/TikTok conversations, paid-ad operations, and voice AI. A Saudi entrant could differentiate with native Arabic dialect handling, Saudi merchant workflows and integrations, local channel coverage, transparent SMB pricing, and unified conversational commerce rather than primarily discovery-data governance.

Vapi developer platform for voice AI agents Usage-based USD

Product Focus

Vapi is an API-first developer platform for building and deploying voice AI agents that make and receive phone calls or run in web applications. Its documented use cases include customer support, sales and lead qualification, appointment scheduling, medical triage, and e-commerce order management, serving developers and enterprise product or operations teams.

Verified Capabilities

  • Inbound and outbound phone calls
  • web-embedded voice calls
  • SMS/chat and web-widget primitives documented in the platform
  • configurable speech-to-text, LLM, and text-to-speech providers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, and others
  • API/database/tool integrations and webhooks
  • multi-assistant orchestration through Squads with context-preserving handoffs
  • outbound campaigns
  • call recording, analysis, monitoring, evaluations, structured outputs, and scorecards
  • multilingual voice configuration with automatic language detection options
  • Arabic listed among supported Vapi Voices V1 languages
  • integrations listed by Vapi include Twilio, Telnyx, Google Calendar, Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, Genesys, Avaya, Zapier, Make, Slack, and GoHighLevel

Pricing

Build: USD $0.05 per call minute plus model-provider costs at cost; SMS/chat USD $0.005 per message plus provider costs at cost; 10 call-concurrency lines included, then USD $10 per line per month; HIPAA add-on USD $2,000/month and Zero Data Retention add-on USD $1,000/month. Scale: annual contract with fixed platform fee, committed volume, and volume-based per-minute pricing; enterprise support and compliance are contact-sales/custom.

Usage-based · USD

KSA Angle

Vapi documents multilingual voice operation and lists Arabic support for Vapi Voices V1, which makes Arabic voice use technically relevant to Saudi and wider MENA deployments. However, its official phone documentation says free numbers are US-only and international/custom numbers require importing a number through Twilio; the reviewed official pages provide no Saudi-specific deployment, local carrier, or MENA case evidence.

Strategic Gap

A Saudi-focused entrant could package Arabic-first merchant operations rather than requiring developers to assemble a voice stack: WhatsApp, Instagram/TikTok messaging, Google reviews, social publishing, ads, and voice in one workflow, with Saudi phone/channel provisioning and local merchant integrations. The reviewed Vapi pages emphasize developer-configurable voice infrastructure and do not list native WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Google Business Profile/reviews, or social-ad workflows, leaving room for a localized turnkey product.

Retell AI voice AI agent platform Usage-based USD

Product Focus

Retell is a platform for building, testing, deploying, and monitoring AI voice and chat agents, with telephony, prompts, tools, knowledge bases, and analytics. Its public positioning centers on businesses and enterprise teams automating inbound and outbound phone operations such as reception, appointment setting, lead qualification, customer service, debt collection, surveys, and call-center workflows.

Verified Capabilities

  • Voice calls, web/in-app chat, SMS, API, SIP trunking and custom telephony
  • node-based conversation flows and single-prompt agents
  • inbound and outbound calling, call transfer, IVR navigation, DTMF capture, real-time function calling, appointment booking, payment processing and record updates
  • website/document knowledge bases with retrieval and website sync
  • CRM integrations documented for Cal.com, HubSpot and Salesforce, plus listed integrations/connectors including Twilio, Vonage, Telnyx, GoHighLevel, n8n, Zapier, Avaya, Genesys, Five9, Amazon Connect, Make and an unofficial WhatsApp connector
  • simulation testing, A/B testing, transcripts, sentiment/success scoring, custom analytics, live monitoring and AI quality assurance
  • configurable language and multilingual-agent support, subject to voice and speech-recognition provider coverage

Pricing

Pay as you go: $0.07–$0.31 per AI voice-agent minute and from $0.002 per AI chat-agent message, with $10 in free credits; enterprise voice and chat pricing is custom/contact sales. Public detailed pricing also lists Retell voice infrastructure at $0.055/minute, platform voices at $0.015/minute, SMS at $0.01/message, and Retell phone numbers at $2/month, with model, telephony and add-on charges varying by selection.

Usage-based · USD

KSA Angle

Retell’s language controls can support multilingual agents when the selected voice and speech-recognition providers cover the language, and its SIP/custom-telephony model can connect to an existing phone system. The reviewed primary pages do not provide specific Saudi customer references, Saudi phone-number availability, Arabic dialect/voice quality claims, local hosting, or Saudi/MENA market positioning.

Strategic Gap

A Saudi-focused entrant could differentiate with Arabic-first, Saudi-dialect voice and text quality; verified local telephony and WhatsApp Business, Instagram/TikTok messaging, Google Business Profile/reviews, social publishing and ads workflows; Saudi data-residency and compliance options; and merchant-native integrations for local commerce, payments, delivery and CRM operations. Retell is a strong voice-agent infrastructure competitor, but its public product surface is not positioned as an all-in-one Saudi merchant communications and growth platform.
