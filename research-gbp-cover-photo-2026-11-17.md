# Google Business Profile - First/Cover Photo Control (as of 17 Nov 2026)

## Executive Summary
Owners and managers of a verified Google Business Profile (GBP) can designate a cover photo and a logo through the Google Maps app (Android/iOS) or through Google Search/Maps on desktop. The single most important caveat, stated by Google itself, is that designating a cover photo does not guarantee it becomes the first image customers see. Google's algorithms choose the displayed image per surface and query, and may substitute a user-submitted photo. In May 2026 Google changed the owner-uploaded gallery to sort strictly newest-first by upload date. There is no longer an employee or photo manager role: only Owner and Manager exist, and both can manage cover photos. Non-owners cannot set a cover; they can post photo updates in Maps, which Google may display and which the owner can only report for removal.

## Key Findings

### 1. Steps to set/change a cover photo and a logo

Desktop/web (Google Search or Maps) - Source 1
1. Open Google Search or Maps on a computer.
2. Search for your business name and city.
3. Click Photos, then Add a cover photo or Add a logo.
4. Select the photo and confirm. To change an existing one, click Change cover or Change logo.
Google exact wording: "To add a cover photo or logo, click Photos > Add a cover photo or Add a logo. To change the cover photo or logo, click Change cover or Change logo."

Android (Google Maps app) - Source 2
1. Open Google Maps; bottom-right tap Business.
2. Tap More, then Cover photo or Logo.
3. Select your photo.
4. Top-right tap Post.

iPhone/iPad (Google Maps app) - Source 3
1. Open Google Maps; bottom-right tap Business.
2. Tap More, then Cover photo or Logo.
3. Select your photo.
4. Bottom-right tap Publish.
Google notes: "If you already have a cover photo or logo, you can update it."

Adding an ordinary business photo: Desktop -> Photos -> Add photos; Android -> Business -> More -> Add photo -> Done -> Post; iOS -> Business -> More -> Add photo -> Done -> Publish. [Sources 1-3]

Removal: Desktop -> Photos -> select photo -> Delete -> confirm; iOS -> Business -> See Business Profile -> Photos -> select -> More -> Delete this photo -> OK. [Sources 1, 3]

Availability: Google's help page is published in ~50 languages, indicating the control is broadly available. [Source 1] I found no official documentation of any business category that cannot set a cover photo. Google notes the logo may not appear unless the profile has name, valid street address, categories, phone number, and opening hours. [Source 1] (Confidence: high for the steps; medium for all-categories/all-regions claim.)

## Conflicting Information

- **Geotagging "near me":** Hundley's 2025 study found a statistically significant improvement for "near me" queries in targeted areas (97% certainty) while Hawkins/Kahlert found no effect and Hundley's own other metrics were flat/negative. All authors agree it is not worth doing; the narrow positive finding is un-replicated. [5][6][11]
- **Does view count influence cover choice?** Practitioners use "likes" as a tactic [10], yet the app resets view counts when re-setting a cover [9], and no source gives a mechanism. Treat as folklore.
- **Website header trick:** Carlile observed instant updates [9]; Hawkins reports it failed for one RV resort [2]; unclear whether GBP website changes still propagate in 2025-2026.
- **"No way to select the default photo" [14] vs. live "Change cover" control [1]:** these refer to different things (forcing the public first image vs. the cover setting itself).

## Confidence Notes

- Only [1] is an authoritative primary source, and even it explicitly disclaims control over the first image. Most operational advice is practitioner consensus from a small, interdependent community (Hawkins, Carlile, and LSF regulars frequently cite each other) - treat as influencer consensus, not independent replication.
- Many core tactic articles are from 2019-2023; 2025-2026 material mostly reaffirms them or covers adjacent AI features. The most recent direct community statement found is Dec 2025 [14].
- Reddit research was thin: the target thread (reddit.com/r/googlebusinessprofile/comments/1sezga5) returned no fetchable content; a search snippet advises setting cover/logo and adding "5-10 more high-quality, very representative business photos," consistent with practitioner advice but not independently verifiable here.
- Search Engine Roundtable items were surfaced via Sterling Sky's 2026 timeline [17] and search snippets; several individual articles were not fetched in full, so their details should be treated as lower-confidence context only.

### 2. Gallery photos vs. uploading new; promoting an existing photo to cover
- Google's own instruction is to "Select your photo" when adding a cover, i.e. pick an image file from the device, which functions as a fresh upload. [Sources 1-3]
- Practitioner guidance (LocalU, Jan 2023): on desktop you cannot promote an already-uploaded GBP photo to cover - you must re-upload it; in the GBP/Maps mobile app you can scroll through existing profile images and choose one as the cover. However, doing so "does not preserve the view count on that image - it's counted as a 'new' image, starts with a zero view count, and becomes a duplicate image in your GBP photos." [Source 8] (Confidence: medium - dated 2023, UI may have shifted; current Google docs do not describe an in-app choose-existing path.)
- Google also notes that if you cannot find an uploaded photo, verify it is not a duplicate and try again. [Source 1]

### 3. Permissions - Owner, Manager, non-owners
- Current GBP has two roles only: Owner and Manager. Site manager was dissolved in 2021, and older granular roles (photo/communications manager) no longer appear in official docs. [Sources 4, 13] There is no employee management role.
- Both Owners and Managers can "Add, delete, and edit cover photos and additional photos" and "Add, delete, and edit logos" - both columns are checked in Google's capability table. [Source 4] Managers differ from owners only in that they cannot add/remove users or remove the profile. [Source 4]
- New owners/managers face a 7-day wait for a few destructive admin actions (delete/undelete profile, remove users, transfer ownership) - photo editing is not listed among the restricted actions. [Source 4]
- Non-owners / public: Any Google user can post a "photo update" on a business's Google Maps listing. Google states: "You can't stop customers from posting photo updates, but you can report inappropriate photos." [Source 5] These posts are not an owner-approval workflow; Google moderates them and may display them. I could not verify any current official feature literally named "Suggest a photo" that routes to owner approval. (Closest verified mechanisms: public photo updates/contributions in Maps, and "Suggest an edit" for profile data.) [Sources 5, 12]
- Can a customer/suggested photo ever become first? Yes, indirectly. Google: "If the cover photo you select is low-quality or if other sources suggest that it's not the best photo to represent your business, a user-submitted photo may be selected instead." [Source 1] Practitioners report Google spontaneously swapping in recent uploads or user photos as the displayed cover. [Sources 7, 8]

### 4. After setting a cover - moderation, delay, replace/remove, limits
- Verification gate: "Your photos and videos show up on Google only after you verify your Business Profile." [Source 1]
- Moderation: Google reviews every upload. Statuses: Pending (upload/processing or unverified - hidden), Not approved (flagged for policy violation - hidden), Live (visible). [Source 1]
- Delay: "It can take up to 24-48 hours before your photos and videos show on your Business Profile." [Source 1] A 2026 secondary source cautions this is a processing guideline, not a publication promise. [Source 10]
- Replace/remove: Use "Change cover" (desktop) or re-select a cover in-app; delete via Photos as described above. [Sources 1-3]
- Counts: The UI exposes a single designated cover photo and a single logo per profile (singular "Add a cover photo"/"Change cover"). I found no official numeric cap on total gallery photos; bulk upload is offered for chains with 10+ locations. [Source 1] (Confidence: medium-high on exactly one cover and one logo; low on any total-photo cap.)
- File specs (official): JPG or PNG; 10 KB-5 MB; recommended 720x720 px; minimum 250x250 px; sharp, well-lit, unaltered. Video: up to 30 s, up to 75 MB, 720p+. [Source 1]
- Cover aspect ratio (not officially specified): Third-party guides commonly recommend 16:9 at 1024x576 px (min ~480x270) [Sources 11, 14]; LocalU says 1024x575 [Source 8]; Sterling Sky recommends 1332x750 and images that work in both landscape and square [Source 7]. These conflict and are industry conventions, not published Google requirements - treat with medium/low confidence.

### 5. "First photo" vs. cover photo; is gallery order controllable?
- Cover does not equal first. Google's direct quote: "Set a cover photo at the top of your profile that best represents your business. In some instances, this action doesn't guarantee the cover photo will show up as the first image for your business." [Source 1]
- Recent change (May 2026): Google's GBP newsletter (quoted by CheckLocalSEO, 19 May 2026): "We've updated Google Business Profile to sort photos and videos by upload date" and "Previously, the gallery order could feel unpredictable. Now, your newest content stays at the top..." This applies to the owner-uploaded gallery, newest-first by Google-recorded upload timestamp. Customer photos live in a separate tab with their own engagement-based ordering, and the Search results thumbnail strip is still separately curated by query relevance. [Source 9] (Confidence: medium-high - reputable source quoting Google's newsletter; I could not retrieve the newsletter itself directly.)
- Practical consequence: You cannot drag-and-drop/reorder gallery photos [Source 16], but after May 2026 you can influence which owner photo sits at the top of the gallery by uploading the desired one most recently. This does not control the Search knowledge-panel image or thumbnail strip. [Sources 9, 10]
- Another nuance: LocalU notes that on some profiles the "see photos" cover image is pulled from Google Image search (e.g. Wikipedia) rather than GBP media; in that case changing the GBP cover will not change the displayed image. [Source 8]

### 6. Edge cases
- One photo vs. many: With a single owner photo, it is generally the pool Google draws from; designating it cover has limited practical effect. With many, Google chooses among owner and visitor media, and (pre-May-2026) order was opaque; post-change, newest owner uploads lead the owner gallery. [Sources 7, 9]
- No cover set: Google selects from whatever media exists for the profile; if owner media is thin or low quality, user-submitted photos may be promoted. [Source 1]
- Google-added / third-party photos (Street View, consumer): These appear in the gallery but are not owner-deletable through the normal Delete flow. Owners can request removal of customer photos via the report flow ("Report inappropriate photos or videos on your Business Profile"); Google reviews and may remove them, and warns that duplicate requests can delay review. [Source 6] Google may show a user-submitted photo first if the chosen cover is poor. [Source 1]
- Removal by Google for policy: A photo flagged as violating policy gets Not approved and is hidden. For restricted media, Google directs UK/EEA businesses to its Business Profile appeals tool and others to Contact Us; one 2026 secondary source reports review/decision can take up to 5 business days and optional evidence must be submitted within 60 minutes of the evidence form opening. [Source 10] (Confidence: medium for the 5-day/60-minute details - secondary source citing Google help; verify against official help before relying on them.)

## Conflicting Information
- Cover resolution/aspect ratio: Google publishes only generic photo specs (min 250x250, recommended 720x720); it does NOT publish a cover-specific ratio. Third parties disagree: 1024x576 (Biziq, Recurpost, SocialBee), 1024x575 (LocalU), 1332x750 (Sterling Sky). No authoritative value can be stated.
- "Suggest a photo": The premise of a named "Suggest a photo" flow with owner approval could not be confirmed in current official docs. Verified reality: public photo updates/posts in Maps, no owner approval, owner can only report. Flag as unverified rather than assert.
- Gallery ordering: Official help says "newest media appears first" (long-standing tip), while CheckLocalSEO frames newest-first sorting as a new May 2026 change replacing unpredictable order. These are not necessarily contradictory (help text may predate/anticipate the UI change), but the precise timing of the behavior change is a secondary-source claim.

## Confidence Notes
- HIGH: All step-by-step UI paths, permissions, file specs, statuses, 24-48 h window, and the "cover not guaranteed to be first" caveat - from Google official help.
- MEDIUM: May 2026 newest-first gallery sort (secondary quoting Google newsletter); existing-photo-to-cover being mobile-only (2023 guide); one cover + one logo per profile (inferred from singular UI labels).
- LOW: Specific cover pixel dimensions; 5-day appeal / 60-minute evidence windows; any claim about category/region-level absence of the control.
